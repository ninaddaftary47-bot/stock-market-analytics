Methodology and data dictionary

Data coverage

8,047 parsed securities, 122,389 annual security observations. Original source contains 5,884 stock files and 2,165 ETF files. Some source files may have been empty or invalid.

Source: user-supplied stocks.zip, etfs.zip, symbols_valid_meta.csv

Daily adjusted price

Adjusted Close is used for price comparisons and annual return calculations, accounting for vendor-provided corporate-action adjustments. Daily and monthly series are embedded for 60 featured securities.

Annual return: (last adjusted close / first adjusted close − 1) × 100

Annualized volatility

Standard deviation of daily adjusted-close percentage returns × √252 × 100. The return is a price-series measure and should not be mistaken for a guaranteed total return.

Trading volume

Sum of reported daily Volume values per security-year. Volume is measured in shares, not USD. Summing volume across securities is a descriptive measure, not a consolidated exchange-volume estimate.

Market breadth

Fraction of eligible securities with a positive first-to-last adjusted-close return in each calendar year. Eligibility filters affect the cross section. The dataset may contain delisted names but coverage is not proven survivorship-bias-free.

Limitations

Coverage ends April 2020; 2020 is a partial year. IPO dates, stale listings, missing trading sessions, unadjusted volume, unequal histories, and vendor adjustment policies limit comparisons. No current quotes or investment advice.
